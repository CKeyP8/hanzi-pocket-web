Editorial method
Common terms, pronunciations and list membership were checked against the
official tables. English definitions are from CC-CEDICT (CC BY-SA 4.0) plus
independently written Hanzi Pocket technical glosses. They are study aids,
not official CSCA translations. See DICTIONARY-LICENSE.txt.
No official examples, textbook chapters, syllabus prose or PDF artwork are
reproduced in the app. Official PDFs are linked for reference.
This is an independent study app with no claimed CSCA endorsement.

Extraction checks
Every source vocabulary row is represented by a card and a level/row reference.
Source numbering typos for foundation 礼物 and 误解 are retained as printed-row
references; stable identity uses row order. An identical repeated 重点 row at a
PDF page boundary is counted once. PH 值 is joined into PH值, and the two
readings of 量 are displayed as liàng / liáng. No vocabulary row is dropped.
The verb 测量 uses the dictionary reading cèliáng in place of the table's
cèliàng. The original vocabulary membership is unchanged.
