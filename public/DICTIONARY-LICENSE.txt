The bundled dictionary.json is a format conversion of the official CC-CEDICT
download. Simplified and traditional character keys share pronunciation and
English-definition alternatives. Definition separators were changed from
slashes to semicolons. No entries were added or editorially changed.
The converted dictionary is distributed under the same CC BY-SA 4.0 license.
The conversion script is included in scripts/build-dictionary.py.
Snapshot downloaded: 2026-10-09.

The application source is separate from the dictionary data. Vocabulary and
definitions entered or edited by the user stay on the user's device.
